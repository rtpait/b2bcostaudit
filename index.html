How does B2B Cost Audit calculate true 12-month payroll and HRIS Total Cost of Ownership?

Unlike generic software directories that only multiply entry base price by headcount, our calculator models base platform fees, per-employee-per-month (PEPM) tiers, per-state monthly tax filing fees, state tax registration fees, January W-2/1099 form charges, international EOR FX spreads, and official 2026 .gov state SUI + federal FUTA taxes.

Why do businesses processing over $25,000/month overpay on Stripe, Square, and PayPal?

Blended flat-rate processors charge 2.9% + $0.30 (plus +0.8% on invoices and +1.5% on international cards) even on regulated bank debit cards capped at 0.05% + $0.22 under Federal Reserve Regulation II (.gov).
